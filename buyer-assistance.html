Viewing & Local Support questions

Can you visit a property when I am overseas?

Ask about a full property and local-area video tour. We agree access with the owner or agent, confirm availability and scope, then provide the agreed video, photographs and factual notes. Please send the property link and location with your enquiry.

What does the property video tour include?

The service can cover the interior, exterior, access, immediate surroundings and local village or area. Additional photographs, selected measurements and a factual follow-up summary are included in the agreed scope. Tell us what you particularly want to see before the visit.

Which areas can I ask about?

We focus on Baza and the surrounding cave-home villages in inland Granada. Tell us whether you are considering Benamaurel, Galera, Castilléjar, Huéscar or another nearby location; availability and travel are confirmed for each request.

How do I arrange a service and find out the cost?

Use the Property Viewing & Local Support enquiry form with the service, location and your timetable. We discuss availability, travel, scope and the price privately before you agree to proceed. Sending an enquiry does not confirm a booking.

Is Property Viewing & Local Support a property survey or legal service?

No. This is practical local assistance, including visits and appointment coordination. It does not replace an independent lawyer, qualified surveyor, valuation or certified translation. The professionals you appoint remain responsible for their advice.
