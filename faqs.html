Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about buying, selling and living in a cave home in Spain.

What is a cave house in Spain?

A cave house is a home built partly or largely into the earth or rock. They are particularly associated with areas of Andalucía and can offer distinctive character and naturally stable indoor temperatures.

Can foreigners buy a cave house in Spain?

Yes. Foreign buyers can purchase property in Spain. The legal and tax process can vary depending on the buyer's circumstances, so independent legal advice is recommended before purchasing.

Where can I find cave houses for sale in Spain?

Cave Homes Spain specialises in distinctive properties in southern Spain, including cave homes around Baza, Benamaurel, Galera and surrounding parts of Andalucía. Available homes can be viewed on the main Properties page.

Are cave houses cool in summer?

The surrounding earth can help cave-home interiors remain relatively cool during hot Spanish summers and moderate temperature changes throughout the year.

Can I renovate a cave house?

Yes, but cave construction requires appropriate materials and techniques, particularly for ventilation and moisture management. Planning permission or other local approvals may also be required depending on the work.

Do cave houses in Spain need an energy certificate?

Spanish energy-certificate requirements depend on the property and circumstances, and some properties may qualify for an exemption. Sellers should establish whether a valid certificate or exemption applies to their particular property.

What should I check before buying a cave house?

Consider independent legal checks covering ownership, registration, planning status, debts or charges and other relevant documentation. For cave properties, also consider physical condition, ventilation, moisture management and proposed renovation work.
