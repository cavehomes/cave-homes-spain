Before you buy

What should you check when viewing a cave house?

Look beyond the appearance of the rooms. Consider ventilation, drainage, signs of damp, the condition of façades and chimneys, access, utilities and the land above or around excavated rooms. Check whether alterations or extensions are reflected correctly in the property's documentation.

Every property is different, so specialist professional advice can be important before committing to a purchase.

Frequently asked questions

Cave-house questions

Are cave houses only found in Granada?

No. Cave dwellings exist in several parts of Spain, but Granada province is especially well known for them. Cave Homes Spain currently has a particular focus on inland Andalucía and the Granada Altiplano.

Can I search by town?

Yes. Use the main property search to filter current listings, and use our local village guides to learn more about individual towns and villages before arranging viewings.

What legal checks should I make?

Buyers should independently verify the property's registered description, ownership, boundaries, planning status, alterations and applicable energy documentation. A qualified legal professional can check the purchase documentation for the individual property.

Can owners advertise directly?

Yes. Property owners can submit a property to Cave Homes Spain for advertising, subject to the site's listing requirements.
