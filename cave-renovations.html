Renovating a cave house can require specialist local knowledge. Cave Homes Spain helps owners and buyers find the right starting point for cave house renovation work in inland Granada.

Before renovation work begins

Cave properties can differ considerably from conventional houses. Before starting structural work or alterations, check what permissions, professional advice and local requirements apply to the particular property.

Important: Cave Homes Spain can help make introductions, but property owners should independently check a contractor’s qualifications, insurance, references, quotations and the permissions required for the work.

Buying a cave home that needs work?

If you are viewing a cave house for sale that needs renovation, consider the condition of the cave, access, utilities, ventilation, drainage and the scope of any proposed alterations before committing to the project.
