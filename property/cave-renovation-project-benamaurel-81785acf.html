About this property

This characterful cave house offers an exciting opportunity to create a spacious and distinctive home in a peaceful hamlet on the outskirts of Benamaurel. It has 11 rooms arranged across two floors, with the possibility of connecting the levels to form one large residence.

The cave requires complete renovation throughout, allowing the new owner to redesign the property according to individual needs. Electricity and mains water are both located nearby and will need to be connected.

The property includes nine ground-floor rooms and three smaller upper rooms, approximately 146.42 m² of excavated cave area, a 32 m² exterior garage and a 90.36 m² front courtyard. It is south facing, dry and ventilated.

The cave enjoys open views across the surrounding countryside and towards the mountains. With its generous accommodation, traditional character and impressive setting, this is an interesting project for creating a permanent home, holiday retreat or rural getaway in Andalucía.
