About this property

Five rooms, mountain views and room to make your own mark.

For €25,000, this cave renovation project near Benamaurel offers a starting point for a home shaped around your ideas. Set in quiet countryside, just five minutes from the nearest village, it combines traditional cave spaces with plenty of land in front and a large field behind.

The cave has five rooms and requires renovation. Imagine a welcoming place to come home to, a few pots by the entrance and somewhere outside to sit and take in the mountain views. These are possibilities to explore as part of the project, rather than finished features.

Water and electricity are nearby; connections will need to be arranged. Buyers should confirm the available services and any permissions needed for their intended renovation.
