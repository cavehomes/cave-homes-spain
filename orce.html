Orce for property buyers

What to know before you view

Orce lies at the eastern side of the Hoya de Baza and is internationally known for the important archaeological and palaeontological sites around the municipality, alongside its traditional Altiplano built environment.

For buyers seeking a quiet village or rural property, compare proximity to services with the privacy of the location. Check road access, utilities, land boundaries and the condition and legal status of older structures, caves, extensions and outbuildings before purchase.

Buyer check: Always verify ownership, registration, planning status, boundaries, alterations and any required energy documentation with appropriate professionals before purchase.

The area

About Orce

Orce lies in the north of Granada province within the Altiplano. Its archaeological heritage is especially important, and the municipality promotes its combination of history, archaeology, nature and tradition.

For buyers

Property & local character

Traditional cave dwellings and cave accommodation are established in and around Orce, making it relevant to buyers exploring the northern Altiplano cave-home market.

As with any rural or unusual property, buyers should independently check title, registration, access, utilities, planning status and any applicable energy documentation before purchase.
