Selling a Cave House in Spain

Good property advertising starts with accurate information, clear photographs and the documents needed to support the details shown to prospective buyers.

Prepare accurate property details

Give buyers a clear description of the home, location, asking price, bedrooms, property type, land and important features. Distinguish existing accommodation from unfinished areas or possible future uses, and avoid presenting planning or business possibilities as guaranteed.

Use strong, representative photographs

Include clear images of the exterior, main rooms, outdoor areas, views and significant features. Current photographs help buyers understand the property before making an enquiry.

Energy information

Provide the property's energy certificate and rating where legally required, or evidence supporting a valid exemption. If confirmation is still being obtained, Cave Homes Spain can keep the property as a draft until the required information is available.

Documents and claims

Owners should be ready to support important statements about the property. Buyers should still carry out their own legal and professional checks before purchase.

Responding to buyers

Clear answers about viewing arrangements, access, utilities and the property's condition can help serious buyers decide whether to arrange a viewing.

Owner declaration. Cave Homes Spain asks owners to confirm that information supplied for advertising is accurate to the best of their knowledge and that required energy information, or evidence of a valid exemption, will be provided.
