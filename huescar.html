Village guide

About Huéscar

Huéscar is a historic northern Granada town surrounded by striking mountain and Altiplano scenery, with strong local traditions, rural life and easy access to the wider cave-home region.

Past & heritage

A little history

Huéscar's heritage includes historic churches, convents, watchtowers, traditional houses, archaeology and rock art. Its location has linked the town to the cultural landscape of the Granada Altiplano for centuries.

Local life

Fiestas & traditions

Important dates include San Antón, the Romería and fiestas of the patron saints Alodía and Nunilón, Corpus Christi and the October fair. The official 2026 local holidays are 25 May and 22 October.
