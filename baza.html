About Baza

One of the historic centres of the Granada Altiplano, Baza combines an old town, surrounding cave districts and a long history shaped by Iberian, Roman, Islamic and Christian periods.

Past & heritage

A little history

Baza has deep archaeological and historic roots and later became an important frontier and market town. Its position in the Hoya de Baza still makes it a practical centre for exploring the cave-home villages of northern Granada.

Local life

Fiestas & traditions

The best-known celebration is Cascamorras in September, shared with Guadix. Baza's 2026 local public holidays are 8 September and 4 December.
