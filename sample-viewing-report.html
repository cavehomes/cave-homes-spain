A clearer picture before you travel.

See how photographs, practical observations and useful questions can come together after an agreed property and area visit.

Illustrative sample — not a completed client visit

Photographs are taken from different published listings to illustrate coverage. The notes below demonstrate the format; they are not findings about one property. Your report would use photographs and observations from your own agreed visit.

SAMPLE-01 · Example only

Example service

Property and local-area viewing

Visit record

Date, location and agreed access recorded in your actual report

01 / A visual record

Your photographs can cover the home, exterior and wider setting, depending on the access and service agreed.

02 / Practical observations

Arrival, access & parking

A real visit records the final approach, road surface, entrance, parking and any steps. Raised outdoor areas are visible in the exterior example; ask for a closer view of the route if mobility or vehicle access matters.

Room layout & everyday use

The interior example shows a kitchen and living area with light from glazed doors. Your video can show how rooms connect and record areas we could not access, rather than leaving you to guess from still photographs.

Nearby amenities & routes

Your report can identify relevant shops, cafés, pharmacies and routes checked during the agreed area visit. Walking or driving times would be labelled and dated. No amenity distances or journey times have been checked for this sample.

03 / Questions to follow up

  1. Is the approach and parking private, shared or subject to permission?
  2. Which water, electricity and internet services are currently connected?
  3. Which furniture, fittings and outdoor areas are included?
  4. Can a second viewing or a qualified professional’s visit be arranged for anything needing closer attention?

04 / A useful follow-up summary

Example summary: “Before travelling, ask the owner to confirm access and connected services. Request a closer video of any steps or rooms important to you, and agree which questions need a further visit or an independent professional.”

What your own report includes

The video tour, photographs, practical summary and follow-up questions included in your quote. An exterior-only visit covers publicly accessible exterior and area views; it does not include interior photographs.

Practical viewing support does not replace an independent survey, valuation or legal advice.
