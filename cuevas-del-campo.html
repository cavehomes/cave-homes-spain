Cuevas del Campo village guide

A northeastern Granada village whose history is closely connected with cave dwellings, farming and the countryside near the Negratín reservoir.

Past & heritage

History & character

The settlement developed from agricultural workers using caves as temporary shelters during harvests; over time these became permanent homes and formed a settled community, historically linked to Zújar.

Local life

Fiestas & traditions

Local celebrations include San Isidro in May, the Fiestas de los Emigrantes in August, the Santo Ángel in October and a living Semana Santa tradition.
