Caniles for property buyers

What to know before you view

Caniles lies in the Baza district and combines a traditional town setting with a large rural municipality, giving buyers a mix of village and country-property options.

Consider how far a property is from everyday services as well as its access, water, electricity and internet arrangements. Rural plots and outbuildings should be checked carefully against the title and cadastral information.

Buyer check: Always verify ownership, registration, planning status, boundaries, alterations and any required energy documentation with appropriate professionals before purchase.

Village guide

About Caniles

Caniles lies just southeast of Baza, where the Hoya de Baza meets the foothills, with rural landscapes, traditional neighbourhoods and centuries of local customs.

Past & heritage

A little history

Archaeological finds around Caniles show very early settlement, including Copper and Bronze Age remains. The municipality's history and countryside make it a useful base for buyers looking beyond the larger town of Baza.

Local life

Fiestas & traditions

San Sebastián is one of Caniles' most distinctive traditions, including the famous 'robo del santo'. Other traditions include Día de la Vieja, Semana Santa and San Antonio. The 2026 local holidays are 20 January and 30 April.

Holiday dates
