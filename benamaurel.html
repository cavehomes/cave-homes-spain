Past & heritage

A little history

Benamaurel has evidence of settlement from prehistoric, Iberian and Roman periods. Its landscape is especially notable for troglodyte housing and medieval excavated architecture, including the Hafas overlooking the Guardal valley.

Local life

Fiestas & traditions

The Moros y Cristianos celebrations in honour of the Virgen de la Cabeza are held around the last weekend of April and include processions, comparsas and the traditional drama known as Los Papeles.
