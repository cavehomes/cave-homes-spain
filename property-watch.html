Cave-home checklist

More than checking the front door.

Rural and cave properties need attention to ventilation, moisture, access and weather-related problems.

Exterior inspection

  • Doors, windows, shutters and gates
  • Visible damage or signs of entry
  • Roof, chimney and exterior cracks
  • Drainage following heavy rain
  • Pipes, tanks, meters and access

Interior inspection

  • Water leaks and unusual smells
  • Damp, mould and ventilation
  • Electricity and agreed appliances
  • Pest activity or visible damage
  • General room condition
