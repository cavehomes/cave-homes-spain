About Cave Homes Spain

Honest, simple and transparent property advertising.

Connecting property owners directly with potential buyers and helping people discover distinctive homes, land and communities across Spain.

Who we are

Cave Homes Spain is an independent property advertising platform created to bring property owners and potential buyers together directly.

We have a particular passion for the unique cave homes of Granada and Andalusia and want to help more people discover this remarkable way of living. Cave Homes Spain is not limited to cave houses: we also advertise cortijos, fincas, traditional houses, apartments and land throughout Spain.

HonestyWe believe property advertising should give buyers clear, accurate information and owners a straightforward way to present their property.
TransparencyWe aim to be clear about what our platform does. We advertise properties and connect people; we do not replace independent legal or professional advice.
SimplicityOur goal is to make discovering and advertising Spanish property accessible, useful and free from unnecessary complications.

Our approach

We believe buyers should be given clear and accurate information about the properties they are considering, and owners should have an accessible way to reach people genuinely interested in Spanish property.

Cave Homes Spain is an advertising and property-discovery platform. We bring buyers and owners together, while encouraging everyone considering a purchase to carry out appropriate independent legal, planning, ownership and property checks before buying.

A selective and honest approach

We are selective about the properties, owners and agents we advertise. We ask sellers to provide clear, accurate information and to be honest about a property's condition, ownership, utilities and any work required.

Although buyers must always carry out their own independent legal and professional checks, our aim is to make searching for property in inland Granada clearer, more trustworthy and less stressful.

Finding trusted local professionals

Buying or renovating a property often requires reliable local knowledge. Where possible, we can help buyers find established local professionals, including independent lawyers, surveyors, architects, builders and specialist cave-home tradespeople.

We aim to suggest people with relevant local experience, but buyers remain free to choose their own professionals and should always check qualifications, independence, prices and references before instructing anyone.

More than property adverts

Our aim is not simply to display listings. We are building a useful resource for people considering buying and living in Spain, with local area guides, information about towns and villages, and practical information about cave-home living.

For individual owners, our goal is to provide greater visibility and a straightforward route to potential buyers.

Our commitment: honest information, selective advertising, practical local support, and a better connection between buyers, property owners and trusted professionals.
