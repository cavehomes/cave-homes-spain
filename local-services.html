Before you book

These are optional paid services. Before you agree, we explain who will provide the work, what is included and excluded, the total price including applicable taxes and travel, timing, payment and cancellation terms. An enquiry alone does not create a booking.

Video visits and local observations are not structural surveys, valuations or legal or financial advice. Appoint your own qualified professionals for those checks. We do not negotiate a purchase or handle purchase deposits as part of these video or area visits.
