Why cave living?

Ancient character. Modern comfort.

Andalucía's cave homes are part of a living architectural tradition. Built into the landscape, their thick earth walls help maintain a steady indoor temperature throughout the year.

  • Naturally insulated in summer and winter
  • Individual rooms full of texture and character
  • Peaceful settings and spectacular landscapes
